\section{The History}

\subsection{Inspiration from Neuroscience and Cognition}

\subsection{From Cybernetics to Artificial Intelligence}
The magical eras of theoretical formulation for computability, intelligence, and artificial intelligence: The 40's (computer, neural networks, cybernetics) and the 50's (artificial intelligence).

Learn useful information: what is predictable (Andrey Kolmogorov, Claude Shannon, later Ray Solomonoff etc...), how to encode and decode information (Information Theory by Shannon), computability (The Von Neumann architecture and the Turing machine), and how to correct errors in prediction via closed-loop feedback (Wiener) and interactive game (von Neumann), and the importance of computational complexity, tractability, and scalability...

\subsection{From PCA to Nonlinear PCA}
Let us switch to a simple but important special case, that has been studied for a long history. It will make the problem of learning much more explicit and tractable. 

The classic model problem of learning a low-dimensional distribution modeled by a linear subspace (PCA) or a nonlinear manifold, denoising, and consistent representation via autoencoding. The important notion of compression and denoising in this classic statistical learning setting... Early roles of a neural network (Hinton's work autoencoding).  

\subsection{Denoising and Diffusion}
Parallel developments in statistical learning and machine learning: How to effectively and efficiently learn a more general distribution that is low-dimensional in a high-dimensional space? The history of denoising, empirical Bayesian denoising in 1950's and 1960's, Tweedie's formula, image denoising in 1990's, score function in early 2000's, and more later... 

\subsection{Early Practice and Evolution of Neural Networks}
The ages of empirical practice: Inception of artificial neural networks in 1943, perceptron and early forms of artificial neural networks in 1960's, and persistent empirical efforts in practicing neural networks from the 1980's till the 2010's (BP, LeNet etc), despite two winters in 70's (XOR) and 2000's (SVM)... 

The use of neural networks as backbones for autoencoding by Hinton, or for independent component analysis by Aapo Hyv\"{a}rinen.

The deep learning revolution started 2010's, and its empirical successes but lack of theoretical explanation.

\subsection{The Rise of Generative Methods}
Learning data distribution via auto-encoding, learning a generative model (a decoder) via discriminative methods such as Tu's work and GAN, auto-regressive, and denoising and diffusion (Chapter \ref{ch:compression}). Many variants of the principle of compression, often in disguise. 

\section{The Present}
\subsection{Explaining Deep Neural Networks}

From the perspective of a new and unifying principle: maximizing information gain, to formulate a new mathematical framework and computational objective for distribution learning and representation learning. In this framework, the role of deep networks is to emulate iterative optimization algorithms in an unrolled fashion (Chapter \ref{ch:representation}) 

\subsection{Differentiating Knowledge and Intelligence}
Clarify the relationship and difference between knowledge and intelligence. The basic characteristics of an intelligent system: the ability to improve existing knowledge and acquire new knowledge by itself.

To ensure the learned internal representation is {\em consistent} with the data distribution (Chapter \ref{ch:autoencoding}) and the learning can be done {\em autonomously} (Chapter \ref{ch:self-consistent}). This entails integrating ideas from closed-loop feedback control and interactive game.

\section{The Future}
\subsection{Towards General Intelligence}
High-level functions such as causal inference, logical inference, and abstract and symbolic computation, which were meant to be studied in the program of ``artificial intelligence.''

The difference between learning and applying such knowledge via external supervision and developing and improving such knowledge autonomously. 

How to systematically and objectively evaluate the level of knowledge and intelligence for any given system? New refined definition of Turing test seems needed.

\subsection{Towards Natural Intelligence}
Discover new computational mechanisms that have enabled the realization of intelligence under natural physical conditions and limitations, without the option of global optimization (such as via BP). Forward optimization and closed-loop error correction?
