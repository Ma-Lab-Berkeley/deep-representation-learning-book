\chapter{Conditional Estimation and Generation}

In the previous chapters of this book, we have studied extensively how to learn a distribution $p(\x)$ of a low-dimensional support in a high-dimensional space. 

Given an observation $\y$ that depends on $\x$,
\begin{equation}
    \y = h(\x) + \boldsymbol{w},
\end{equation}
how to solve the ``inverse problem'' of obtaining a good conditional estimate $\hat \x(\y)$ for $\x$ or generating a sample $\hat{\x}$ that is consistent with the observation $\y \approx h(\hat{\x})$. To accomplish either task well, we essentially need to get a hold of the conditional distribution $p(\x \mid \y)$.
