\chapter*{Acknowledgment}
This book is primarily based on research results that have been developed within the past eight years. Thanks to the generous startup funds from UC Berkeley (2018) and the University of Hong Kong (2023), Yi Ma was able to embark on and dedicate to this new exciting research direction in the past eight years. Through the years, related to this research direction, Yi Ma and his research team at Berkeley have been supported by
\begin{itemize}
    \item The multi-university {\em THEORINET} project  for the Foundations of Deep Learning, jointly funded by the Simons Foundation and NSF DMS (grant \#2031899);
    \item The {\em Closed-Loop Data Transcription via Minimaxing Rate Reduction} project funded by ONR (grant N00014-22-1-2102); 
    \item The {\em Principled Approaches to Deep Learning for Low-dimensional Structures} project funded by NSF CISE (grant \#2402951).
\end{itemize} 
This book would have not been possible without the support for these research projects. We have also drawn tremendous inspiration from research results of students and colleagues who are involved in these projects.
